Support Ticket Routing and Headcount Recommendation

### Short answer

I would recommend NOT assigning the two recommended hires to Billing until you get an opportunity to review them. The numbers indicate that the wrong tickets are likely adding to Billing's backlog. Prioritize the bot's routing rules and then take a look at the workload before hiring.

### What the data says (January 2025 – June 2026)

Tickets analyzed: 11641.

Billing accounts for 20.8% of tickets and Logistics accounts for 16.4% of tickets as routed.
By customer message: Billing (15.3%) and Logistics (25.6%) are the most frequented categories.
In the Billing-tagged tickets queue 36.1% or 875 are delivery related tickets. These should be assigned to Logistics, except in special cases.
The misrouted Billing tickets have a 36.5% breach rate while the other Billing tickets have a 10.2% breach rate.
The number of transfers is about 0.9 per ticket for misrouted Billing tickets and about 0.14 per misrouted ticket in other Billing tickets in helpdesk data.

The findings indicate an association between incorrect routing with higher SLA violations and increased handling.

### Recommended goal

Lower tickets incorrectly assigned to Billing from Billing queue by delivery by more than 36.1% to less than 10%. Update the bot's rules for how deliveries / tracking problems are directed to Logistics.

### Estimated financial impact

The additional cost for misrouted tickets is estimated at around **₹326/- per misrouted ticket** as the difference in cost of breach-credit and transfer observed.

If it is assumed that 650 tickets are being misrouted per week, the annualised estimate would be around **₹8.28 lakh**.
Assuming an average of about 150 tickets per week at the export, the figure works out to be a rough estimate of **₹1.91 lakh per year**.

These are only projections, not guaranteed savings. These are actual weekly volumes and will need to be confirmed prior to making a budget decision.

### Validation and limitations

The validation sheet for Excel is now showing:

* Intent accuracy: **47/50 (94%)**
* Team accuracy: **46/50 (92%)**
The intent and team correct out of the tickets we reviewed: **45/48 (93.8%)**
* Original chatbot team-tag accuracy: **27/50 (54%)**

I also manual-checked the labels on the tickets and resorted to the AI to assist with the checking. The results are based on a small sample and are not to be considered as a human validation of every ticket. The transfer data is only available for the helpdesk period starting in September 2025.

### Recommendation

Approve the bot-routing improvement, and review volumes and SLA performance in Billing and Logistics after 1 month, and put off hiring decision until after the effect of the routing change has been measured. At this stage the data is not conclusive evidence either way for either team.
